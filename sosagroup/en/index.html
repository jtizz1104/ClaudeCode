About us

Leading SosaGroup with a calling to serve

Keeping the same philosophy and calling to serve with which her mother, Sulma Paredes, founded the organization, Gloria Sosa made SosaGroup the leading organization in paperwork for Paraguayans abroad and for foreigners who want to come to Paraguay, whether to live, to invest or to grow their business.

An accountant by training (National University of Asunción, UNA), Gloria Sosa created the Procedures Manual that ensures SosaGroup's maximum effectiveness in every procedure we carry out for our clients.

When she took over the organization, Gloria developed a working method that made it possible to broaden the reach and professionalize the services her mother had started through sheer effort, out of her natural calling to help people.

Today she is joined by her daughters Silvana (business administration graduate and real estate advisor) and Valeria (economist and financial advisor).

Our story A true “pay it forward” story From a very young age I watched my mother, Sulma Paredes, helping people in one way or another. Read the full storyHide the story

Most of the time there were strangers in our house: people my mother invited in because they had come from the countryside and had to travel on, to the capital or to other cities. They almost always had a long wait for the bus that could take them, or had surgery or an appointment at the Regional Hospital, or some other situation. Our house was right in the center of the city of Encarnación.

I remember all the times we had to “add more water to the stew” because there were more mouths to feed! That was a constant throughout my life, and I am truly grateful to my mother for instilling in us that spirit of service and love for others!

So when she, a teacher by calling and an old-school educator, went through all the paperwork for her retirement and moved to Asunción, since several of us children were studying at university there, some people started asking her to handle their paperwork too.

More requests followed, given how expensive it was to travel to Asunción to file or collect documents at different offices, and given that most of these procedures are not completed in a single day. Above all, they came because of the complete trust that everyone who knows her has in her.

From that moment on, what began as a chain of favors now lets us make a living doing what we love: serving people.

Requests kept growing, and it became impossible for my mother to keep doing it all on her own. That is when I joined the business, wrote a Procedures Manual covering every procedure we handle, with costs and timelines, and gave it to an IT professional to design the system. Then we added the first version of the website, the blog and the Facebook page.

Thanks to the magic of the internet, today we have many foreign clients and Paraguayans living abroad, who make up most of our client base.

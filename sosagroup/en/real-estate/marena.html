The project

Marena is a residential development by Eydisa located in Luque, near the Asunción city line, presented as Paraguay's first urban Crystal Lagoons lagoon. The masterplan comprises 8 towers around a crystal-clear lagoon with a beach, in a green setting, 2 km from Parque Ñu Guasu and 3 km from Asunción's corporate hub. It offers studios and 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom units from 35.80 m² to 120.22 m² of total private area, with covered parking and balcony. The project features over 22,000 m² of amenities, a shopping center and 24/7 security. It also includes Marena Rentals, a short-term and long-term rental management service for owners.

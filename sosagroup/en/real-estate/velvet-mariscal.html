The project

Velvet Mariscal is a mixed-use tower located in Barrio Mariscal, Asunción, next to Shopping Mariscal, in an area with two malls that welcome up to 25,000 people per day. The building features a commercial ground floor, four parking levels, an amenities floor, floors designed for Airbnb-style rentals, residential floors and penthouses with terraces on the top floors. Units range from 30 m² studios to 224 m² 3-bedroom apartments with terrace. It offers residents-only amenities, shared amenities and amenities exclusive to the Airbnb floor. It is developed by Building Innovations and managed by BI Propiedades.

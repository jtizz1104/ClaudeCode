The project

Sanmar Aurora is a 9-level residential tower located in the San Jorge neighborhood of Mariano Roque Alonso, on Avda. Soldado Robustiano Quintana & Fulgencio R. Moreno. It offers studios from 28.15 m², 1-bedroom apartments from 40.30 m² and 2-bedroom apartments from 65.03 m². Units include an open kitchen, air conditioners, kitchen cabinets and closets, and bathrooms with shower screens and mirrors. Its amenities include coworking, barbecue area, gym, terrace, lounge, laundry and 24-hour security. The project was designed by Julia Galanti Arquitecta and built by Gómez Abente Construcciones.

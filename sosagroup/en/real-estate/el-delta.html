The project

El Delta is a complex of condominiums, common spaces, green areas, commercial and dining zones and a corporate center, all part of 1,600 hectares on the right bank of the Paraguay River. It brings together several developments: La Isla del Delta (66 lots from 1,200 m²), Barrio Norte (300 lots with 28 ha of lagoons), Barrio Náutico (68 ha with beach and nautical canal), Terrazas Malibú (30 lagoon-front residences with a dock), Riverside Zafiro / Las Terrazas del Delta (46 apartments from 283 to 710 m²) and Río Verde (2- and 3-bedroom en suite apartments). It features entrance gates with 24-hour security, a drinking water and wastewater treatment plant, underground wiring and a sustainable irrigation system. It is complemented by the Marina del Delta, the Tarumá Golf course, Las Palmas Street Mall, a corporate district and a free trade zone.

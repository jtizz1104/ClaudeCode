The project

Riverside Zafiro is part of Riverside – Las Terrazas del Delta, a complex of five buildings with direct access to the Paraguay River, private marinas and a golf course, designed by Estudio Gómez Platero. It offers 3- and 4-bedroom en suite apartments, from 283 m² to 710 m², with panoramic terraces, floor-to-ceiling windows and a grill on the balcony-terraces. Ground-floor units have their own private garden, and the level 7 penthouses include a barbecue area and pool. Finishes include central VRV climate control, quartz countertops, large-format porcelain tiles, elevators with private landings and a generator with 100% coverage.

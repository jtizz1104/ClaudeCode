The project

Alban TRIUM is a 15-floor, 12,200 m² building developed by Viur Desarrollos in Asunción's corporate and commercial hub, near Shopping del Sol, Paseo La Galería and WTC Asunción. The ground floor houses retail spaces and a triple-height restaurant; floors 1 and 2 are parking and floor 3 is home to the amenities. Floors 4 to 14 offer studios and 1- and 2-bedroom apartments, while floor 15 is reserved for 2- and 3-bedroom penthouses. Units include a glass-ceramic cooktop, built-in oven, granite countertops, closets and air conditioning in main areas. The project is designed for traditional or short-term rentals, with operations managed by VIUR Hospitality.

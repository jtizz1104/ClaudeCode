The project

Studio Recoleta is a ROOTZ Desarrollos Inmobiliarios project in Asunción's Recoleta neighborhood, geared toward investment and first-time homebuyers. The building has 8 levels of apartments, plus a basement, ground floor and rooftop. It offers 27.5 m² studios, 35 m² studio PLUS units and 1-bedroom apartments from 44.7 m², designed for short-term rental or owner occupancy. The ground floor includes an entrance lobby, restroom and coworking, while the rooftop brings together a climate-controlled barbecue area, terrace, pool with sun deck, gym, locker rooms and coworking. It is located about 300 meters from Shopping Mariscal López.

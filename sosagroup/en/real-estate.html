    Frequently asked questions

    Before you start

    Can I buy a property if I don't have residency yet?

    It depends on your situation and the type of transaction. In the consultation we review your case and, where appropriate, coordinate the purchase with your residency application.

    Do I need to be in Paraguay during the process?

    Much of the search and the visits can be done remotely. Some stages, such as signing, may require you to be present or to grant a power of attorney; we confirm this for your case.

    What do you check before I sign?

    We review the property's documents and the terms of the transaction, and explain each point before you make a decision.

    How much does the service cost?

    It depends on the type of transaction. We tell you the scope and terms before we start, with no obligation.
